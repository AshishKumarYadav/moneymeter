MoneyMeter

Privacy policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

MoneyMeter is an expense tracker that works on your phone. Your financial data is stored on your device and is never sent anywhere. The only thing the app sends is an anonymous crash report if it crashes, and you can turn that off.

Stays on your phone

Saved in a local database on your device only.

Nothing is shared

Your transactions and messages never leave the phone.

No account

No sign-up, no login, no profile.

What the app reads

  • SMS messages from banks and payment services, to find transactions: the amount, the card or account, the merchant and the reference number. Messages from people are skipped by checking the sender.
  • Entries you add yourself: cash payments, notes, subscriptions and spending limits.

Where your data goes

Your SMS, transactions, amounts, names, notes and limits never leave your phone. There are no accounts, analytics or advertising.

Your data is kept in the app's private storage and is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Crash reports

If MoneyMeter crashes, it sends an anonymous crash report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics so the problem can be fixed. A report contains the error, the app version, the phone model and Android version, a random installation ID, and which screen or feature was in use. It never contains your messages, transactions, amounts, names, merchants or card numbers.

Crash reports are on by default. Turn them off in the app under Privacy policy → Send crash reports. Google processes reports on our behalf under the Firebase terms and keeps them for 90 days.

App updates

When a new version is available, the Google Play Store app on your phone may offer it inside MoneyMeter. That check is made by Google Play, not by MoneyMeter, and does not include any of your data.

Children

MoneyMeter is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them. It collects no personal information from anyone; crash reports are anonymous.
